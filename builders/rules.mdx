---
title: "Hard rules"
description: "What a builder cannot soften after activation"
---

You choose parameters inside the allowed bands. You cannot rewrite floors, splits, or close economics after the account is live.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Ordinary profit | `builderPoolBps` to builder, residual to trader. Vault and protocol take 0 |
| Forfeited close surplus | Builder 40% / protocol 20% / LP residual |
| Profit lock | 30% of accepted peak profit, on both lifetime and daily floors |
| Withdrawal bar | 3 profitable checkpoint days (≥ 0.70% growth) **or** profit ≥ 3% of `A` |
| Checkpoint | 1 day |
| Sponsored term | 30-day subscription |
| Daily drawdown | Mandatory on sponsored accounts, absent on self-funded |
| Who can sponsor | Anyone with `R + P`. No registry, no stake |
| Markets | Live MainVault whitelist. Edit does not close the account |

## Trailing floor

```
A = $1,000, D = 10%, accepted peak = $1,200
baseFloor  = $900
profitLock = $60
trailing   = $960
```

Intraday peaks move only after a 1% acceptance gate. Checkpoint accepts any higher lifetime peak. After a proven profit claim, peaks reset to `A`.

## Daily drawdown

Not a flag you pass at activation. If you sponsor another trader, Daily DD is on at half of `D`. If the trader self-funds, it is off.

Same example, day start `$1,000`, daily peak `$1,200` → daily floor `$1,010`. Effective floor is the max of the two.

## TARGET

`targetProfit` is on-chain. Zero disables it. The `A + targetProfit` threshold blocks ordinary withdrawal. The relayer writes `TargetReached` and keeps the ordinary split.

## Whitelist

The live list is the trading rule for every Active account. Unauthorized size is flattened. Remaining live markets stay tradeable. Emergency whole-account close is Admin, and Admin **keeps** ordinary close profit.

## What you still own

How you select traders, what you charge them off-protocol, and how you brand the program stay yours. Once `activateFunded` lands, capital protection is the protocol’s.
