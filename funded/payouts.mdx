---
title: "Profit and payouts"
description: "How profit is withdrawn and when the account closes"
---

Ordinary profit is a two-way split. Vault and protocol take none of it. The split is pinned at activation and the firm cannot change it later.

```
builderShare = floor(profit × builderPoolBps / 10_000)
traderShare  = profit − builderShare
```

`builderPoolBps` is 10%-70%. 20/80 is a common product term, not a protocol constant.

On self-funded both buckets hit the same address. The trader gets 100%.

Protocol and LPs earn from activation premium, subscription and forfeited surplus. Not from a keep-profit withdrawal.

## Withdraw profit

Profit is `currentEquity − A`. High-water mark is not the basis.

All of this must hold:

- account Active, term still live
- no pending trader-close intent
- TARGET not reached
- profit at least $1
- account flat
- enough tradeable Core USDC

Then either:

- 3 profitable checkpoint days (equity grew at least 0.70% vs the previous checkpoint)
- or immediately: profit ≥ 3% of `A`

The request snapshots the whole profit and bridges Core → EVM. After arrival, `claimWithdrawal` pays the ordinary split: trader residual from the clone, builder share to the pull ledger.

After a proven claim, peaks reset to `A` and the profitable-day streak restarts. A rejected Core send can be cancelled. Stored peaks do not weaken.

On a one-day self-funded term the first checkpoint is at expiry. In-term withdrawal is the 3% path only.

<Info>
On a sponsored account the owner can cancel the subscription. Close happens at the next qualifying checkpoint and keeps the ordinary split.
</Info>

## Close

One reason, written once. Floors decide when. They are not a fee. Settlement uses snapshotted `Q` only.

| Reason | Unwithdrawn close profit `max(Q − A, 0)` |
|---|---|
| Drawdown | Forfeited |
| TraderRequested | Forfeited |
| TARGET | Ordinary split |
| Subscription expired | Ordinary split |
| Subscription cancelled (sponsored) | Ordinary split |
| Admin | Ordinary split |

Profit already in a pending withdrawal stays vested. It never mixes into `Q`. Even on a forfeiting close that piece still splits by ordinary `builderPoolBps`.

### Forfeited surplus

```
builder    = 40% of (Q − A)
protocol   = 20%
vault / LP = residual
trader     = 0
```

Unused reserve returns to `reserveRecipient`. Direct sponsor: the builder. Self-funded: the trader. Only actual principal loss consumes reserve.

### Voluntary close

A flat owner can request close. That is intent, not the reason. After the proxy drains positions and orders, the relayer commits `TraderRequested`. If profit can still be withdrawn, do that first.

## Settlement and claims

```
principalLoss   = max(A − Q, 0)
reserveConsumed = min(R, principalLoss)
reserveRefund   = R − reserveConsumed
closeProfit     = max(Q − A, 0)
```

Settlement credits fixed recipients. It does not push USDC.

Anyone can call `claimPayout(recipient)`. Funds go only to that address. Several accounts can aggregate on one recipient.

The vault share of premium, subscription and forfeited surplus stays in NAV. It does not enter the ledger.

<Warning>
A forfeiting close zeros unwithdrawn close profit. Withdraw and claim first if the account is still eligible.
</Warning>
