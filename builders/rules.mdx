You pick parameters inside the bands. After activation, floors, splits and close economics are locked.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Ordinary profit | `builderPoolBps` to builder, residual to trader. Vault and protocol take 0 |
| Forfeited surplus | Builder 40% / protocol 20% / LP residual |
| Profit lock | 30% of accepted peak profit, lifetime and daily |
| Withdrawal | 3 profitable checkpoint days (≥ 0.70%) or profit ≥ 3% of `A` |
| Checkpoint | 1 day |
| Sponsored term | 30-day subscription |
| Daily drawdown | On for sponsored, off for self-funded |
| Who can sponsor | Anyone with `R + P` |
| Markets | Live MainVault whitelist. Edit does not close the account |

## Trailing floor

```
A = $1,000, D = 10%, accepted peak = $1,200
baseFloor  = $900
profitLock = $60
trailing   = $960
```

Intraday peaks move after a 1% gate. Checkpoint accepts any higher lifetime peak. After a proven profit claim, peaks reset to `A`.

## Daily drawdown

Not a flag at activation. Sponsored: on, at half of `D`. Self-funded: off.

Same example, day start `$1,000`, daily peak `$1,200` → daily floor `$1,010`. Effective floor is the max of the two.

## TARGET

`targetProfit` is on-chain. Zero disables it. `A + targetProfit` blocks ordinary withdrawal. The relayer writes `TargetReached` and keeps the ordinary split.

## Whitelist

The live list is the trading rule. Unauthorized size is flattened. Remaining live markets stay open. Admin close keeps ordinary close profit.

Selection, brand and what you charge off-protocol stay yours.
