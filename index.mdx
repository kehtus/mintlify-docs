![Proportion overview](/images/image.jpeg)

Proportion is on-chain prop-trading infrastructure on Hyperliquid. Traders get funded accounts on real capital, without a custodial firm login. Builders run their product on a stack that already has capital, a risk engine and settlement. LPs deposit USDC into a shared vault and earn from activation premium, subscription and forfeited surplus. Yield is not fixed and can be zero.

## Context

Hyperliquid and builder codes created demand for trading capital on-chain. The same dynamic that produced thousands of forex prop firms is moving here.

Unlike a classic prop firm, an on-chain version has to be non-custodial, checkable and composable. Proportion is that layer. You do not write your own contracts or your own risk engine.

## Mission

Create an environment where each side takes their profit on terms that are transparent to everyone. Give traders what they deserve, what prop firms promised them.
