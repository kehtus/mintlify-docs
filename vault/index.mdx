---
title: "How It Works"
description: "What the vault holds, what limits allocation, and what limits exit"
---

You deposit USDC into `MainVault` and get LP shares. The vault uses that money as the trading capital of every funded account, keeps each backer's buffer separate from LP money, and settles every account when it closes.

## Flows

| Flow | What happens |
|---|---|
| Funding an account | The vault puts the account's trading capital on HyperCore. That capital is LP money; the backer pays the buffer `B` and the premium `P` on top of it. See [Account Parameters and Fees](/backers/fees) |
| Fees | Half of each activation premium and each subscription payment goes to the vault and raises the share price. See [Yield and Risks](/vault/yield) |
| Settlement | When an account closes, the vault takes the account's funds back from HyperCore and records what each party is owed. See [Profit and Payouts](/funded/payouts) |

While an account trades, the risk engine starts closing it when equity falls below the floor. The floor starts at account size minus the backer's buffer and can rise from there. It sets when a close starts, not the price the close fills at.

You hold shares, and everything you earn or lose shows up in their price. Fees push it up. A loss bigger than a backer's buffer pulls it down at settlement, and any repayment of the resulting [vault debt](/vault/yield#vault-debt) slowly pushes it back up. There is nothing to claim separately.

Until an account settles, the vault counts it at its original size, so open gains and losses do not move the share price you deposit or exit at.

The owner may lend idle USDC to Hyperlend, and the interest also raises the share price.

## Allocation limits

| Limit | Current setting | What it limits |
|---|---|---|
| Max utilization | **80 %** | capital allocated to funded accounts, against the vault's assets |
| Max single account | **10 %** of the vault's assets, capped at **\$20,000** | the size of one funded account |
| Vault cap | **\$25,000** | the vault's assets |

The account size and drawdown a backer chooses inside these limits are on [Account Parameters and Fees](/backers/fees).

## Exit

An exit needs no notice and has no lock-up, but it depends on liquidity and utilization. A withdrawal goes through only if utilization stays at or below the ceiling afterwards, and only from USDC the vault does not already owe for buffers, refunds and payouts. If part of the money sits in Hyperlend, the vault takes what is missing from there. If Hyperlend cannot pay it back, the withdrawal fails.

A backer with live funded accounts exits on the same terms as any other LP. With utilization at the ceiling, a large withdrawal can go through only after an account settles.

<Warning>
A pause stops all LP withdrawals. The owner or the relayer can pause; only the owner can unpause. Trader and backer payout claims keep working through a pause.
</Warning>

## Governance

The owner can change the account-size and drawdown ranges, the utilization and per-account ceilings and the vault cap, but every change waits 72 hours in the timelock. The two ceilings have fixed bounds in the contract, and drawdown cannot go above 50 %. Account size and the vault cap have no fixed upper limit.

The premium tiers, the profit-split range, the forfeiting split and the subscription multiplier are written into the code, so changing any of them takes an upgrade. Upgrades of MainVault wait 72 hours in the timelock. See [Security](/security).
