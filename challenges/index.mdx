A challenge is an escrowed evaluation. A builder publishes a plan with its price and terms, a trader buys it, and the builder's share of the payment stays locked in `ChallengeVault` until the challenge ends. Passing does not fund by itself: a challenge ends in one of five outcomes recorded on-chain, and the one a trader wants is a promotion into a funded account on the terms pinned at purchase.

## What is on-chain today

What is on-chain today is the money and the outcome.

- **Publishing.** Any wallet holding shares in the vault can publish a plan: allocation, drawdown, profit split, target and price. A plan cannot be edited once published, only disabled. There is no registration and no approval.
- **Purchase.** The trader pays in USDC. The protocol and referral cuts are taken at checkout, and the builder's net is locked against that challenge. A sale funds its own lock, so the builder posts nothing up front.
- **Expiry.** A challenge runs 30 days from purchase. After that, anyone can expire it.
- **Outcome.** While the challenge is live the builder promotes, rejects or refunds it, and the trader can forfeit. Each outcome moves the locked money one way and is final.

| Outcome | Who | What happens to the money |
|---|---|---|
| Promoted | Builder, or the protocol owner | The lock pays the funded account's `B + P`, the builder's wallet covers any shortfall, and a funded account opens on the pinned terms |
| Rejected | Builder | The trader gets nothing back. The lock is released to the builder |
| Refunded | Builder | The builder's locked net is paid back to the trader. The protocol and referral cuts are not |
| Forfeited | Trader | Same as rejected |
| Expired | Anyone, after 30 days | Same as rejected |

A promotion is atomic: the lock is released, `MainVault` collects `B + P`, and the funded account exists with its buffer, or the whole transaction reverts. Unused buffer from that account returns to the builder's balance in `ChallengeVault` when it closes.

## What is not on-chain

The trading. Today the evaluation runs off-chain, on a simulated account that is opened from the on-chain purchase and marked against a live price feed. The account is held to the same floors as a [funded account](/funded/rules), and the plan sets the rest: the profit target, one step or two, and whether profitable days are required. Reaching the target does not pass the challenge by itself. The chain never sees the exam equity and records no pass or fail, so the grade is the builder's decision to promote. A stranger can verify that the payment was locked and where the money went. They cannot verify the grade.

## What we are building

A challenge type whose result can be verified on-chain, so that the promotion follows from the record rather than from the builder's decision. That closes the remaining ways an evaluation can be gamed. The escrow, the pinned terms and the promotion path above stay as they are.

## Without an evaluation

A funded account can also be opened without an exam through a finance listing on the same vault. A trader seeking a builder, or a builder seeking a trader, posts pinned terms and any escrow, and a counterparty fills it by paying the rest of `B + P`. The fill opens the funded account in the same transaction. A listing lives three days, and the creator cannot fill their own.
