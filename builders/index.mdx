Earn from funding traders, without building the stack for it.

You pay reserve `R` plus premium `P`. MainVault allocates principal `A` and settles one close path. Selection, brand and off-protocol price stay yours.

`builder` is whoever paid `R + P`. Anyone with USDC for the charge can open an account, for themselves or someone else. No vault deposit.

<Warning>
Hyperliquid builder-code fees from a funded account go to `protocolFeeReceiver`, not the builder.
</Warning>

## Earnings

Builders do not take subscription. That split is 50/50 vault / protocol.

Volume is bounded by the vault: 80% utilization, one account at most 10% of NAV. First loss is reserve `R`.
