---
title: "Challenges"
description: "Escrowed evaluations, backer decisions and the path to a funded account"
---

A challenge is an escrowed evaluation. A backer publishes a plan, a trader buys it, and the backer's share of the payment stays locked until the challenge ends. Passing does not open a funded account by itself: the challenge has to be promoted onchain. Account size, drawdown and profit split are fixed at purchase, and the funded account's profit target is chosen at promotion.

## What the contracts record

The contracts record the payment, its lock and the final outcome.

- **Publishing.** A backer must hold vault shares, at least the minimum if one is set. The plan sets the account size, drawdown, profit split, evaluation terms and pricing. Once published, it cannot be edited, only disabled. No registration or approval is needed.
- **Purchase.** The backer must still hold the shares when the trader buys. The USDC price comes from the plan and current settings. Protocol and referral cuts are taken at checkout, and the backer's net is locked against that challenge.
- **Expiry.** A challenge runs 30 days from purchase. After that, anyone can expire it.
- **Outcome.** Before day 30, the backer can promote, reject or refund the challenge. The trader can forfeit it until it has an outcome. Each outcome settles the lock and is final.

| Outcome | Who | What happens to the money |
|---|---|---|
| Promoted | Backer, or the vault owner | The lock joins the backer's balance, `B + P` is paid from it, and the backer's wallet covers any shortfall. A funded account opens |
| Rejected | Backer | The lock goes to the backer |
| Refunded | Backer | The lock goes back to the trader. The protocol and referral cuts are not returned |
| Forfeited | Trader | Same as rejected |
| Expired | Anyone, after 30 days | Same as rejected |

A promotion either opens the funded account in full or changes nothing. Whatever is left after `B + P` stays with the backer. When that account closes, its unused buffer returns to the backer's balance, less any [vault debt repayment](/backers/fees#vault-debt-repayment) on a forfeiting close.

## What is not onchain

The evaluation runs offchain, on a simulated account marked against market prices. It uses the trailing and daily floors from [Funded Account Rules](/funded/rules). The plan sets the evaluation target, one step or two, and whether profitable days are required. Reaching the target does not pass the challenge by itself. The chain records no pass or fail, so promotion is up to the backer, or the vault owner. Anyone can check that the payment was locked and where it went, but the chain alone does not show whether the trader met the rules.

## Without an evaluation

A funded account can also be opened without an exam, through a finance listing. A trader looking for a backer, or a backer looking for a trader, posts fixed terms and optional escrow. Whoever fills it pays any shortfall against the current `B + P`, excess escrow returns to the creator, and the funded account opens in the same transaction. Unused buffer belongs to the named backer, no matter who supplied the escrow.

A creator can have one open listing at a time, and it stays open three days. They cannot fill their own, but they can cancel it; once it expires, anyone can return the escrow to them.
