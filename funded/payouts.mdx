title: "Payouts"
description: "How profit is withdrawn, how close pays, and how claims are pulled"
---

Ordinary profit is a two-way split. Vault and protocol take **none** of it.

```
builderShare = floor(profit × builderPoolBps / 10_000)
traderShare  = profit − builderShare
```

`builderPoolBps` is pinned at activation, in **10% – 70%**. Typical product terms use 20% builder / 80% trader. That is a chosen term, not a protocol constant.

On a self-funded account both buckets hit the same address, so the trader receives 100% of ordinary profit.

<Info>
Protocol and LPs earn from activation premium, subscription, and forfeited close surplus. Not from a keep-profit withdrawal.
</Info>

## Withdraw profit

Profit is `currentEquity − A`. High-water mark is not the basis.

A request needs all of:

- account Active, subscription / term still live
- no pending trader-close intent
- TARGET not reached
- profit at least **$1**
- account flat (no open margin or notional)
- enough tradeable Core USDC

Then **either**:

- **3** profitable checkpoint days, where a day counts if equity grew at least **0.70%** versus the previous checkpoint, **or**
- instant: profit **≥ 3% of `A`**

The request snapshots the whole profit and bridges Core → EVM. After arrival, `claimWithdrawal` pays the ordinary split. The trader residual is sent from the clone; the builder share is credited to the pull ledger.

After a proven claim, peaks reset to `A` and the profitable-day streak restarts. A rejected Core send can be cancelled without weakening stored peaks.

On a one-day self-funded term the first checkpoint is due when the term lapses, so an in-term withdrawal is the **3% instant** path only.

## Close

One close reason is written once. Floors decide **when** closing starts. They are not a fee. Settlement uses snapshotted Core trading capital `Q` only.

| Reason | Unwithdrawn close profit `max(Q − A, 0)` |
|---|---|
| Drawdown | Forfeited |
| TraderRequested | Forfeited, voluntary surrender |
| TARGET | Ordinary split |
| Subscription expired | Ordinary split |
| Subscription cancelled (sponsored) | Ordinary split |
| Admin | Ordinary split |

`UnauthorizedAsset` and `LeverageBreach` still exist on the enum for ABI stability. The live clone does not produce them. A whitelist mismatch is flattened, not closed.

A profit already accepted into a pending withdrawal stays **vested** under every later close reason. It is never mixed into `Q`. Even on a forfeiting close that piece still splits by ordinary `builderPoolBps`.

### Forfeited surplus

```
builder   = 40% of (Q − A)
protocol  = 20%
vault / LP = the residual, including dust
trader    = 0
```

Unused reserve still returns to `reserveRecipient`. On a direct sponsor that is the builder. On self-funded that is the trader. Only actual principal loss consumes reserve.

### Voluntary close

A flat owner can request close. That records intent; it does not write the reason yet. After the proxy drains and the account is flat and order-free, the relayer commits `TraderRequested`. Eligible profit should be withdrawn and claimed **before** that request.

## Settlement and claims

```
principalLoss   = max(A − Q, 0)
reserveConsumed = min(R, principalLoss)
reserveRefund   = R − reserveConsumed
closeProfit     = max(Q − A, 0)
```

Settlement credits fixed recipients. It does not push USDC to the trader, builder, or protocol.

Anyone may call `claimPayout(recipient)`. Funds can only arrive at that address. Several accounts can aggregate under one recipient.

The vault share of premium, subscription, and forfeited surplus does not enter the ledger. It is already in NAV.

<Warning>
A forfeiting close zeros unwithdrawn close profit. Withdraw and claim first if the account is still eligible.
</Warning>
