A trader-financing protocol on HyperEVM.

Traders run funded accounts on Hyperliquid. Builders write the terms. LPs deposit USDC into MainVault. Yield is not promised and can be zero.

A `FundedAccount` is isolated capital under pinned rules. Fills are real Hypercore executions. The trader operates that account. They do not own the vault and cannot take principal home.

MainVault allocates principal and settles one close path. The account trades only perps on the live whitelist. Drawdown, TARGET, time, withdrawals and claims are contract state.
