> Capital on Hyperliquid's own book, under contract rules.

Proportion allocates you capital in an onchain funded account, and your orders go to the real Hyperliquid book. The contract fixes your terms at activation and checks its rules against your equity on HyperCore. When the account settles, your payout is credited to your address, and nobody has to approve it before you collect.

Whoever funds the account posts a drawdown buffer, so if the account ends in a loss, that buffer covers it before LP capital does.

## How to get a funded account

Anyone can fund anyone. Whoever funds you is your backer: a prop firm, an individual or you yourself. The backer opens the account for you and sets its terms: the size, the drawdown and your share of profit. Some backers run a [challenge](/challenges) first, some activate an account directly. How you qualify is between you and the backer, not the protocol.

You can also fund yourself, posting the buffer and the premium. The account is then self-funded: one wallet is both backer and trader, so on an ordinary close both shares of the profit stay with you.

Either way the account is assigned to your EVM address on HyperEVM. For supported actions on the Proportion frontend you only sign: the relayer sends the transaction and pays the gas.
