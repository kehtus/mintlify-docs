title: "Onboarding"
description: "How to open the first funded account"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wallet">
    Any EVM wallet on HyperEVM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activation">
    `activateFunded(trader, accountSize, drawdownBps, builderPoolBps, targetProfit, proposedReferrer, feeBps)`. You pay `R + P`. The vault places principal `A`. The caller becomes `record.builder`. Sponsored `feeBps` is 200 only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If `trader` is you, the path is self-funded: same call, tiers 200 / 400 / 800. Neither path needs a vault deposit.

There are no delegates. Gasless goes through the ERC-2771 Forwarder: the signer pays USDC and becomes the builder.

`proposedReferrer` binds the caller, not the trader.

Once the account is open, it belongs to the trader's EVM address. Until a withdrawal or a voluntary close, the trader does not have to send transactions: risk sync, checkpoint and flattening are done by the protocol. Withdrawal, claim, subscription cancel and voluntary close need the trader's signature.

<Warning>
If you also deposit USDC as an LP, that is a separate role. Shares do not open or cap activation. They take residual vault income and can fall.
</Warning>
