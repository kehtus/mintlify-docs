<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate">
    `activateFunded(trader, accountSize, drawdownBps, builderPoolBps, targetProfit, proposedReferrer, feeBps)`. You pay `R + P`. The vault allocates `A`. The caller becomes `record.builder`. Sponsored `feeBps` must be 200.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If `trader` is you, that is self-funded: same call, tier 200 / 400 / 800. No vault deposit on either path.

Gasless goes through the ERC-2771 Forwarder. The signer pays USDC and becomes the builder.

`proposedReferrer` binds the caller, not the trader.

<Warning>
An LP deposit is a separate role. Shares do not unlock activation. They take residual vault income and can fall.
</Warning>
