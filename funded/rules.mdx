---
title: "Funded Account Rules"
description: "The limits your funded account is held to, and every reason it closes"
---

Your account size, your drawdown percentage and your profit split are set by your builder at activation, and nothing changes them afterwards. You can read your floor on-chain at any moment, and the contract closes the account by that same number.

Your equity is read from HyperCore precompiles, which the contract uses directly as its oracle. An off-chain risk engine samples the account and calls the contract, but it can only trigger what the contract's rules already allow: it works through API wallets and never holds your funds.

## Limits

| Rule | Description | Consequence |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Trailing drawdown** | A floor below your account size, which rises as you lock in profit | Account closes, unwithdrawn profit forfeited |
| **Daily drawdown** | Half the trailing percentage, measured from the day's start. Sponsored accounts only | Account closes, unwithdrawn profit forfeited |
| **Leverage** | A cap per market, taken from the live protocol list | Order refused |
| **Markets** | Only perps on the live protocol list | Order refused |
| **Subscription** | 1.5 % of account size every 30 days, taken from your equity. Sponsored accounts only | If it lapses, the account closes on the ordinary split |

<Note>
Daily drawdown and the subscription apply only to sponsored accounts. A self-funded account prepays a fixed term instead, and it does not renew.
</Note>

## The floor

Your floor starts at account size minus your drawdown percentage. As you make money, 30 % of your peak profit above account size is locked into it, so the floor follows you up. It does not come back down until you claim a withdrawal, which resets it to account size.

It moves during the session, not once a day. A new peak counts once it is at least 1 % higher than the last accepted peak, and the floor rises with it. Profit you have held for an hour has already raised your floor. Give it all back and the account can close before the day ends.

On a sponsored account a second floor runs from the day's opening equity, at half the trailing percentage, with its own 30 % lock on the day's peak. Whichever floor is higher is the one that applies. The two are never added together.

Equity has to fall strictly below the floor to breach it. Touching it exactly is not a breach.

## Markets and leverage

Trading is allowed on the perps the protocol lists, at that market's leverage cap. Both are checked when the order is placed, so an order outside them is simply refused. Neither costs you the account.

If a market is removed from the list while you hold a position on it, that position is closed out and the rest of your account keeps trading.

## Profit target

Some accounts carry an absolute profit target. Reaching it closes the account on your ordinary split. It is not a breach and nothing is forfeited.

<Warning>
Once equity is at the target, ordinary withdrawals are refused and the account is closed on the ordinary split. You keep the profit but lose the account. If you want to keep trading it, take profit out before you reach the target.
</Warning>

## What is allowed

Copy trading, bots and EAs, and news trading are all allowed.

<Tip>
Everything not forbidden on this page is allowed. Trading style, trade frequency, holding overnight or through weekends: none of it is restricted within the listed limits.
</Tip>

## What closes the account

Two closures forfeit whatever profit is still in the account: a drawdown breach, and asking to close it yourself. Four do not: the subscription expiring, cancelling the subscription, an admin closure, and reaching the profit target.

Profit you have already requested for withdrawal is vested and survives all six. See [Profit and Payouts](/funded/payouts).
