title: "Yield and risks"
description: "Where LP yield comes from, and what can hit vault capital"
---

Vault income is residual. It can be zero.

## Yield sources

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Activation premium" icon="percent">
    50% of `P`. `P` is a share of account size, not of reserve
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscription" icon="repeat">
    50% of sponsored renewals (1.5% of `A` per period). Self-funded prepaid premium splits the same way
  </Card>
  <Card title="Forfeited surplus" icon="chart-line">
    Residual `Q − A` after 40% to the builder and 20% to the protocol, on Drawdown or TraderRequested
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
LPs do not take ordinary trader or builder profit. Unused reserve `R` returns to the builder (or the trader on self-funded), not the vault.
</Info>

Loss above reserve becomes `reserveDeficit` in NAV. Settlement still completes. First loss is `R`. Anything beyond `R` hits NAV.

## Risks

Putting capital in the vault means share value can move.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Correlated drawdowns" icon="waves">
    Premium `P` is small relative to allocated `A`. Simultaneous breaches can eat accumulated vault income and push `sharePrice` below deposit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Gap and slippage" icon="zap">
    Floors start close. They do not guarantee a fill at the floor. Liquidation or a gap can print `Q` below the floor. Everything above `R` hits LPs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Originations quality" icon="users">
    LPs do not pick traders and do not set activation parameters. Builders do. A weak sponsor hits the pool in proportion to allocated principal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Smart contract" icon="file-code">
    MainVault and FundedAccount have been independently reviewed. Residual implementation risk stays.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hyperliquid / Hypercore" icon="link">
    Equity, fills and bridges depend on Hypercore reads. Failures there show up as vault capital.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LP exit" icon="lock">
    Shares are not locked by open accounts. Exit is still cut by utilization: you cannot pull cash so that `totalAllocated` exceeds 80% of the new NAV.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Governance" icon="settings">
    Account-size, drawdown and utilization corridors can move through `setRiskConfig` inside published bounds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
