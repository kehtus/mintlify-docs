## Why it exists

- Opaque rules and blocked payouts: floors, splits, and claims are contract state
- Custodial "funded" logins: capital sits in an isolated clone, not a firm demo
- Every firm writing its own engine: one vault, one risk path, one settlement
- Fragmented LP capital: one MainVault instead of a pool per program
- Yield sold as APY: vault income is residual and can be zero

## What each role gets

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Trader">
    - Isolated FundedAccount on Hypercore, allowlisted perps
    - A builder opens the account, or the trader self-funds
    - Ordinary profit splits by `builderPoolBps` (10–70%). Product terms often use 20% builder / 80% trader. That is an activation parameter, not a protocol constant. Self-funded: both buckets go to the trader
    - The firm cannot rewrite the floor or deny a payout the contract already credited
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Builder">
    - You write the terms. The contracts enforce them
    - Pay reserve `R` plus premium `P`. Unused reserve returns at close
    - Ordinary profit share is `builderPoolBps`. Forfeiting close: 40% of actual `Q − A`
    - Any wallet with USDC for `R + P` can open an account for themselves or someone else. A vault deposit is not required. `builder` is whoever paid
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="LP">
    - Deposit USDC into MainVault. Shares track conservative NAV
    - Income: 50% of activation premium, 50% of subscription, residual forfeited surplus
    - LPs do not take ordinary trader / builder profit. Unused `R` is not theirs
    - Yield is not fixed
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
