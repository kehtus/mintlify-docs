## Problems Proportion Solves

- **Opaque rules and blocked payouts** in classic prop — rules and splits are encoded in the protocol, with payouts handled through the on-chain flow
- **B-book conflict of interest** — builders and the protocol are aligned with trader profit, not trader losses
- **Counterparty risk** — funded accounts are non-custodial, and protocol rules prevent arbitrary payout denial by the prop firm
- **High barrier to launching a prop firm** — Proportion provides capital, risk enforcement, and settlement without requiring builders to run their own contract stack
- **Fragmented onchain capital** — one Vault coordinates liquidity instead of separate pools for every firm
- **Lack of DeFi access to prop-industry economics** — Vault shares create a composable primitive for LPs

## What Each Side Gets

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Trader">
    - Capital access without KYC or artificial rules
    - A-book execution on real Hyperliquid liquidity — real trades with real capital
    - A profit split fixed in the contract at activation, between 30 % and 90 % to you, and unchangeable afterwards
    - Nobody can refuse the payout: settlement credits your address, and the release call pays that address and no other
    - The account is yours to trade within the protocol's rules, and the firm that issued it cannot block a withdrawal
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Builder">
    - Infrastructure for funded accounts: capital from the Vault, on-chain risk enforcement, and settlement
    - No registration, no minimum stake, no quota — any wallet holding USDC for the activation charge can fund a trader
    - You post a reserve as first-loss capital, and whatever the trader does not lose comes back to you
    - Revenue on three levels: your share of trader profit, 40 % of profit forfeited on a breach, and whatever margin you price into your own evaluation
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="LP">
    - Yield from the ecosystem: half of every activation premium, half of every subscription, and the Vault's residual when an account forfeits
    - Your capital is not first in line for a loss — each account carries a builder's reserve that is spent before LP principal, though a loss larger than that reserve still reaches you
    - No need to pick builders or traders — sharePrice reflects Vault P&L and can increase or decrease
    - Composable shares (potentially for DeFi integrations)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
Losses larger than a builder's reserve do reach LPs. What each flow pays, and what it costs when an account overshoots its floor, is on [Yield and Risks](/vault/yield).
</Note>
