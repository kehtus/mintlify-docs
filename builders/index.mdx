> The builder owns the product. Proportion provides the funded layer.

For a builder, the funded account is the most capital-intensive and infrastructure-heavy part of a prop firm. Proportion provides that layer: capital, on-chain risk enforcement, and settlement. Evaluation, brand, marketing and trader selection stay your product. Proportion is API-compatible with Hyperliquid Builder Codes, so it fits into an existing trading frontend with little client-side change.

You buy capital from the protocol and pay for it at activation with `R + P`.

## What Proportion gives you

An SDK call creates an isolated on-chain account with capital, a drawdown limit and automatic risk enforcement. The account belongs to the trader within the protocol's rules.

| Your product | The protocol |
|--------------|--------------|
| Evaluation and selection | Capital from the LP vault |
| Trader onboarding | Drawdown floors enforced on-chain; markets and leverage enforced at order entry |
| Brand, marketing, pricing | Settlement: fees, subscriptions, and the profit split |

Boundary: everything before activation is handled by the builder; everything after activation is enforced by the protocol.

## Who counts as the builder

Whoever paid `R + P`. There is no registration, no minimum stake and no funding quota. Funding yourself uses the same call with your own address as the trader, so one wallet is both backer and trader. Your profit share and, on a forfeiting close, your share of the forfeited profit are both recorded against that address. The first loss is the reserve `R`, not a second deposit in shares.

## Where your revenue comes from

| Source | What it is |
|---|---|
| Your share of trader profit | `builderPoolBps`, fixed per account at activation. You take that share of every profit withdrawal and of an ordinary close. See [Profit and Payouts](/funded/payouts) |
| Forfeited profit | If the account closes on a drawdown breach or a trader's own request, you take **40 %** of whatever profit it returns |
| Evaluation margin | Whatever you charge for selection, above the activation charge you pay the protocol. See [Challenges](/challenges) |
| LP yield, if you want it | Depositing into the vault is optional and independent of funding traders. See [Yield and Risks](/vault/yield) |

Two things are not builder revenue. The subscription splits between the vault and the protocol. The Hyperliquid builder-code fee on a funded account is approved for the protocol's fee receiver, not for you. Unused reserve does come back, but that is your own capital returning rather than income.
