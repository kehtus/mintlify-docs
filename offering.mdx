## What it solves

- **Opaque rules and blocked payouts.** Floors, splits and claims live in the contract. A firm cannot deny a payout the protocol already credited.
- **Custodial "funded" logins.** Capital sits in an isolated clone, not a firm demo.
- **Every firm writing its own engine.** One vault, one risk path, one settlement.
- **Fragmented capital.** One MainVault instead of a pool per program.
- **Yield sold as APY.** Vault income is residual and can be zero.

Vault and protocol do not take ordinary trader profit. They earn from activation premium, subscription and forfeited surplus at close.
