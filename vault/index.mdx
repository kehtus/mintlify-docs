MainVault takes LP deposits, allocates principal to funded accounts, books builder reserve and settles close.

## Conservative NAV

```
grossAssets = EVM USDC + totalAllocated

totalAssets = grossAssets
            − active builder reserve
            − claimable reserve refunds
            − claimable payouts

sharePrice  = totalAssets / totalShares     // 1 USDC if no shares
```

Reserve, unpaid refunds and unpaid claims do not lift share price. Deposits and exits use this on-chain state until close realizes `Q`.

## Limits

| Parameter | Default | What it limits |
|---|---|---|
| Max utilization | **80%** | `totalAllocated` vs NAV |
| Max single account | **10%** / $20k | One `A` |
| Account size | $10-$20,000 | Per-account `A` |
| Drawdown | 5%-25% | Trailing `D` |

Activation also needs NAV ≥ $100 at defaults (`minAccountSize / maxSingleAccountBps`). Deposits work below that.

## Exits

`withdraw(shares)` is immediate. `requestWithdraw` / `executeWithdraw` is an optional 1-day cooldown on the same accounting.

Exits spend only EVM cash above reserve, refund and payout liabilities. Utilization must stay intact. Open funded accounts do not lock shares.

`deposit(amount, proposedReferrer)` can bind the LP referral on first touch. `depositFor` never binds.

## Governance

Risk bands, utilization and caps move through `setRiskConfig`. Fee tiers and 50/50 splits are constants.
