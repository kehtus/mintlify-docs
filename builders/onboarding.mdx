<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a wallet">
    Any EVM wallet on HyperEVM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold USDC for R + P">
    The activation charge is the reserve `R` plus the premium `P`. There is no separate deposit and nothing to register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the account">
    One call opens the account for your trader, and whoever paid is recorded as its builder. Funding yourself is the same call with your own address as the trader.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Skin in the game

The first loss is the reserve `R`. It is not a second deposit and not an LP cushion.

- You pay a reserve to fund the account. Unused reserve is returned when it closes.
- A loss larger than `R` lands on the LP vault as `reserveDeficit`, not on you.
- Depositing into the LP vault is optional. Shares earn and lose across the whole pool, and they neither grant nor withdraw your ability to fund traders.
- The premium `P` is charged on every activation and is not returned.

<Warning>
`R` is your capital at risk. If your trader gives back the account's designed drawdown, you lose the reserve. That is the point of it, and it is why the protocol does not need to approve you.
</Warning>

## Onboarding a trader

The account is issued to the trader's EVM address on HyperEVM. On the Proportion frontend they pay no gas and need no HYPE: every operation, the profit withdrawal included, is relayed for them. In practice a trader need not touch the chain at all until they take money out.

That keeps your product simple. The trader pays for whatever selection you run, passes it, and receives an account, without having to understand gas, wallets or signing.
