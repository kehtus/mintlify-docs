A funding protocol that gives market exposure to the trader and to the prop firm, on clear rules.

Floors, splits and claims are contract state. Capital sits in an isolated clone, not a firm demo. One vault, one risk path, one settlement. Vault income is residual and can be zero.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Trader">
    - Isolated FundedAccount on Hypercore. Whitelisted perps.
    - A builder opens it, or you self-fund.
    - Ordinary profit splits by `builderPoolBps` (10-70%). Self-funded: both buckets go to you.
    - The firm cannot rewrite the floor or deny a credited payout.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Builder">
    - Your traders, your terms, your brand.
    - Pay reserve `R` plus premium `P`. Unused reserve returns at close.
    - Ordinary share is `builderPoolBps`. Forfeiting close: 40% of `Q − A`.
    - Anyone with USDC for `R + P` can open an account. No vault deposit. `builder` is whoever paid.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="LP">
    - Deposit USDC into MainVault. Shares track conservative NAV.
    - Income: 50% of premium, 50% of subscription, residual forfeited surplus.
    - Unused `R` is not yours. Yield is not fixed.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
