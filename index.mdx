Proportion is a prop-trading protocol on Hyperliquid. Funded accounts trade real capital on the Hyperliquid order book, so trader profit comes from the market, not from a firm.

LPs supply the capital through an onchain vault. A backer opens each account and posts a buffer that takes losses before LP capital does.

## What you can verify onchain

Funded accounts run on Hyperliquid's own chain: the contracts on HyperEVM, the trading on HyperCore.

- **Deposits.** LP deposits, vault shares and backer buffers are recorded in the vault contract. [How the vault works](/vault)
- **Activation.** The payment and the account's terms are recorded in one transaction. Size, drawdown, profit split and target cannot change afterwards. [Funded account rules](/funded/rules)
- **Trading.** Every order, fill and position is recorded on HyperCore, Hyperliquid's onchain order book, under the account's public address.
- **Risk checks.** The account contract reads its equity from HyperCore and computes its floors. Anyone can start closing an account that falls below its floor.
- **Settlement and payouts.** A loss comes out of the backer's buffer first, then LP capital. Profit is split by the terms fixed at activation, and each share can be claimed without anyone's approval. [Profit and payouts](/funded/payouts)

Proportion is non-custodial: funds are held by the contracts, not by the team. The risk engine and the exchange proxy trade through standard Hyperliquid API wallets, which can place and cancel orders but cannot withdraw or transfer funds. [Security](/security) covers custody and the audit.
