<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a wallet">
    Any EVM wallet on HyperEVM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold USDC for B + P">
    The activation charge is the buffer `B` plus the premium `P`. There is no separate deposit and nothing to register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the account">
    One transaction opens the account for your trader, and the wallet that paid becomes its backer. To fund yourself, name your own address as the trader.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your buffer

Your buffer `B` takes the trader's losses first.

- Unused buffer comes back when the account closes, less any [vault debt repayment](/backers/fees#vault-debt-repayment) on a forfeiting close.
- A loss larger than `B` lands on the LP vault, not on you.
- You do not need LP shares to activate an account. Publishing and selling [challenge plans](/challenges) does require them, and a minimum stake if one is set.
- You pay the premium `P` on every activation, and it is not returned.

<Warning>
`B` is your capital at risk. If the trader's loss reaches the full drawdown, you lose the whole buffer.
</Warning>

## Onboarding a trader

The account opens on the trader's EVM address on HyperEVM. The trader authorizes orders and account requests with their wallet.

Passing your evaluation does not open the account by itself: you decide whom to fund.
