Vault yield is residual. It can be zero.

## Income

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Activation premium" icon="percent">
    **50%** of `P`. `P` is a share of account size, not of the reserve
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscription" icon="repeat">
    **50%** of sponsored renewals (1.5% of `A` per period). Self-funded prepaid premium uses the same 50/50
  </Card>
  <Card title="Forfeited surplus" icon="chart-line">
    Residual of `Q − A` after builder 40% and protocol 20%, on Drawdown or TraderRequested
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
LPs do not receive ordinary trader / builder profit. Unused reserve `R` returns to the builder, not to the vault.
</Info>

A loss beyond reserve becomes `reserveDeficit` in NAV. Settlement still completes.

There is no builder stake. First-loss is reserve `R`. Loss beyond `R` lands in NAV.

## Risks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Correlated drawdowns" icon="waves">
    Premium `P` is small relative to allocated `A`. Simultaneous breaches can consume more than accumulated vault income and push `sharePrice` below the deposit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Overshoot / gap risk" icon="zap">
    Floors start a close. They do not guarantee a fill at the floor. A Core liquidation or gap can realize `Q` below the floor. Excess beyond `R` hits LPs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Issuance quality" icon="users">
    LPs do not pick traders. A builder who sponsors poorly affects the pool in proportion to allocated principal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Smart-contract risk" icon="file-code">
    MainVault and FundedAccount have been independently reviewed. Residual implementation risk remains.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hyperliquid / Hypercore dependency" icon="link">
    Equity, fills, and bridges depend on Hypercore reads and Hyperliquid infra. Faults there show up as vault capital.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LP exit" icon="lock">
    Shares are not locked by open accounts. Exits still cannot break utilization: `totalAllocated` must stay within 80% of post-withdraw NAV.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Governance" icon="settings">
    Account-size, drawdown, and utilization bands can move through admin `setRiskConfig` inside published bounds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
