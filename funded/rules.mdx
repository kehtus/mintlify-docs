title: "Funded Account Rules"
description: "The limits your funded account is held to, and every reason it closes"
---

The contract fixes your account size, drawdown and profit split at activation. Your floor is stored onchain, and the contract checks your equity against it.

The contract reads your equity straight from HyperCore. An offchain risk engine watches the account and calls the contract to enforce these rules. A separate exchange proxy signs your orders. Neither holds the account's funds.

## Limits

| Rule | Description | Consequence |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Trailing drawdown** | A floor that starts below account size and rises with your equity peaks | Forfeiting close |
| **Daily drawdown** | Half the trailing percentage, measured from the day's start. Backed accounts only | Forfeiting close |
| **Leverage** | A cap per market | Order refused |
| **Markets** | Only perps on the live protocol list | Order refused |
| **Subscription** | 1.5 % of account size per 30-day renewal, paid from profit. Backed accounts only | If it lapses, an ordinary close |

<Note>
A self-funded account prepays a fixed term instead and does not renew automatically. You can buy more time before it ends, as long as the new end date is within 30 days of today.
</Note>

## The floor

Your trailing floor starts at account size minus the drawdown buffer. For every dollar of profit above account size it rises by only 30 cents, so the gap between your peak and the floor widens by 70 cents. It does not fall with equity. A completed profit withdrawal resets the peak to account size, and the floor drops back to its starting level.

On a \$10,000 account with a 10 % drawdown, the floor starts \$1,000 below the peak; after a peak of \$15,000 it sits at \$10,500, which is \$4,500 below the peak.

Backed accounts also have a daily floor. It starts at the day's opening equity minus half the drawdown percentage and rises by 30 % of the day's peak profit. The higher of the two floors applies, never their sum.

Equity has to fall strictly below the floor to breach it. Touching it exactly is not a breach.

## Markets and leverage

You can trade the perps the protocol lists, up to each market's leverage cap. Both are checked when you place an order, so an order outside them is refused and the account carries on.

If a market is removed from the list while you hold a position on it, that position is closed out and the rest of your account keeps trading.

## Profit target

Some accounts carry a fixed profit target. With a target, you cannot withdraw profit while the account is running. When equity reaches account size plus the target, the account closes on your ordinary split. If the floor is breached at the same time, the drawdown close takes priority.

## What is allowed

Copy trading, bots, EAs and news trading are all allowed.

<Tip>
Everything not forbidden on this page is allowed: trading style, trade frequency, holding overnight or through weekends.
</Tip>

## What closes the account

A drawdown breach and closing the account yourself are forfeiting closes: the profit still in the account is split between the backer and the protocol. Four closes are ordinary and pay your usual share: the subscription or prepaid term running out, cancelling the subscription, an admin closure, and reaching the profit target.

Profit you have already requested for withdrawal is kept for you on all six. See [Profit and Payouts](/funded/payouts).
