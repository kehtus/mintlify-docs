> Capital and strong execution on Hyperliquid, without KYC or artificial rules.

Proportion gives you capital through an onchain funded account on Hyperliquid. Your orders reach the real Hyperliquid book, and the rules you trade under are enforced by the contract rather than interpreted by a firm.

The reserve behind your account is the backer's own money, and it is spent before anyone else's — so losing the account costs them. That holds until you are well ahead: once profit lifts your floor above the account size, a breach returns their reserve whole and pays them a share of what you forfeit. It is the one case where a closure pays them and not you, and it is the argument for taking profit out rather than letting it ride.

<Tip>
Your profit share is written into the contract at activation and cannot be changed afterwards. Nobody can refuse the payout: settlement credits your address, and the release call pays that address and no other.
</Tip>

## How to get a funded account

Accounts are issued by the firms building on Proportion. Each runs its own selection, and how you qualify is part of that firm's product rather than the protocol's.

You can also fund your own account through the same call, putting up the charge yourself. One wallet is then both backer and trader, and the whole ordinary split stays with you.

Either way the account is issued to your EVM address on HyperEVM. You do not need to hold HYPE — the protocol covers gas for every operation, including the withdrawal — so there is nothing for you to sign on-chain until you take money out.
