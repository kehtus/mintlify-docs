The center of Proportion is the MainVault contract. LP deposits, funded-account principal, builder reserve accounting and the single close path all meet here. Anything that moves capital in the system goes through the vault.

## Conservative NAV

An LP's share is represented by vault shares. Accrued vault income lifts `sharePrice`. You do not claim it separately.

```
grossAssets = USDC on EVM + totalAllocated

NAV = grossAssets
    − active builder reserve
    − claimable reserve refunds
    − claimable payouts

sharePrice = NAV / share count
```

Reserve, unpaid refunds and unpaid payouts do not lift share price. Until close locks `Q`, deposits and exits use this on-chain NAV.

## Allocation limits

| Parameter | Default | What it caps |
|---|---|---|
| Max utilization | 80% | `totalAllocated` vs NAV |
| Single account | 10% / $20k | One `A` |
| Account size | $10-$20,000 | One `A` |
| Drawdown | 5%-25% | Trailing `D` |

Activation also needs NAV ≥ $100 at defaults (`minAccountSize / maxSingleAccountBps`). Deposits below that still work.

## Exits

Ordinary `withdraw(shares)` is immediate. `requestWithdraw` / `executeWithdraw` is an optional 1-day pause on the same accounting.

You can only spend EVM cash above reserve, refunds and payouts. Utilization after the exit must stay inside the cap. Open funded accounts do not lock shares.

`deposit(amount, proposedReferrer)` can bind an LP referral on first touch. `depositFor` never binds.

## Governance

Account-size, drawdown and utilization corridors move through `setRiskConfig`. Fee tiers and the 50/50 splits are constants.
