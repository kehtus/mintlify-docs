description: "The limits your funded account is held to, and every reason it closes"

Your account size, your drawdown percentage and your profit split are fixed at activation and nothing changes them afterwards. The floor your account is held to is readable on-chain at any moment, and the code that returns it is the code that acts on it.

## Limits

| Rule | Description | If it is hit |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Trailing drawdown** | A floor below your account size, which rises as you lock in profit | Account closes, unwithdrawn profit forfeited |
| **Daily drawdown** | Half the trailing percentage, measured from the day's start. Sponsored accounts only | Account closes, unwithdrawn profit forfeited |
| **Leverage** | A cap per market, taken from the live protocol list | Order refused |
| **Markets** | Only perps on the live protocol list | Order refused |
| **Subscription** | 1.5 % of account size every 30 days, on a sponsored account | Account closes on the ordinary split |

<Info>
At activation the firm issuing your account sets its size, the drawdown percentage and the split. Daily drawdown and the renewing subscription both apply to sponsored accounts only. A trader funding their own account prepays a fixed term instead, and it does not renew.
</Info>

## The floor

Your floor starts at account size minus your drawdown percentage. As you make money, 30 % of the profit above account size is locked into it, so the floor follows you up and never comes back down while the account is open.

It moves during the session, not once a day. A new peak counts as soon as it clears 1 % above the last accepted one, and the floor rises with it — so profit you have held for an hour is already partly protected, and giving it all back can close the account before the day ends.

On a sponsored account a second floor runs from the day's opening equity, at half the trailing percentage, with its own 30 % lock on the day's peak. Whichever floor is higher is the one that applies. The two are never added together.

Equity has to fall strictly below the floor to breach it. Touching it exactly is not a breach.

## Markets and leverage

Trading is allowed on the perps the protocol lists, at that market's leverage cap. Both are checked when the order is placed, so an order outside them is simply refused — neither costs you the account.

If a market is removed from the list while you hold a position on it, that position is closed out and the rest of your account keeps trading.

## Profit target

Some accounts carry an absolute profit target. Reaching it closes the account on your ordinary split — it is not a breach and nothing is forfeited.

<Warning>
Reaching the target also ends ordinary withdrawals. Once equity reaches the target a withdrawal request is refused, and the account is closed out on the ordinary split. If your account has a target, take your profit out before you reach it.
</Warning>

## What Is Allowed

| Activity       | Status   |
|----------------|----------|
| Copy Trading   | Allowed  |
| Bots & EAs     | Allowed  |
| News Trading   | Allowed  |
| Information-based Trading | Allowed |

<Tip>
Everything not forbidden on this page is allowed. Trading style, trade frequency, position size, holding overnight or through weekends — no restrictions within the listed limits.
</Tip>

## What closes the account

Two closures forfeit whatever profit is still in the account: a drawdown breach, and asking to close it yourself. Four do not: the subscription expiring, cancelling the subscription, an admin closure, and reaching the profit target.

Profit you have already requested for withdrawal is vested and survives all six. See [Profit and Payouts](/funded/payouts).
