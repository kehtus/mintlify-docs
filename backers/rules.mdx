---
title: "Protocol Rules"
description: "What the protocol enforces on a funded account, and which rules stay yours"
---

You set the account size and the drawdown percentage at activation. From then on the protocol enforces the floor, the allowed markets and the leverage caps, and neither you nor the trader can soften them.

## What the protocol enforces

| Rule | On breach |
|---|---|
| Trailing floor | The account closes and your buffer absorbs the loss |
| Daily floor, backed accounts only | Same |
| Markets and leverage | The order is refused. The account keeps trading |
| Subscription | The account closes on the ordinary split |

## How drawdown sets the floor

The trailing floor starts at `A − B` and rises by 30 % of the peak profit above `A`. A completed profit withdrawal resets it to `A − B`. A worked example is on [Account Parameters and Fees](/backers/fees#how-drawdown-sets-the-floor), the trader's view on [Funded Account Rules](/funded/rules).

## Daily drawdown

Every account you back also has a daily floor, which you do not set: the day's opening equity less half the drawdown percentage, plus 30 % of the day's peak profit. A self-funded account has none. The higher of the two floors applies.

The daily floor also limits what one day can take from your buffer. If the day opens at `A`, the floor starts about half of `B` lower, so a close that settles near it consumes about half of `B`. A gap or liquidation can still consume the whole buffer in one day, and anything beyond `B` lands on LPs.

## Markets and leverage

Markets and leverage limits are the same as for the trader: see [Funded Account Rules](/funded/rules).

## What closes an account

Two closes are forfeiting: a drawdown breach and the trader asking to close. Four are ordinary: the subscription expiring, the trader cancelling it, an admin closure, and a profit target being reached.

On those four your unused buffer comes back in full. On the two forfeiting closes the vault may keep part of it while it is in debt; see [Vault debt repayment](/backers/fees#vault-debt-repayment). The amounts are on [Account Parameters and Fees](/backers/fees), the trader's side on [Profit and Payouts](/funded/payouts).

## The rules you keep

[The evaluation](/challenges) stays yours: its price, its rules and trader selection. The funded account enforces only the protocol's rules, so if you promise stricter limits, you enforce them yourself.

One of your rules can sit onchain: a profit target set at activation closes the account on the ordinary split as soon as the trader reaches it.
