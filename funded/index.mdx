> Real Hyperliquid execution. Rules and payouts live in the contract, not in a firm back-office.

A trader gets an on-chain funded account on Hyperliquid: deep liquidity, no custodial firm login, no hidden terms after activation. Fills are real capital on the real Hyperliquid book. That is checkable at every step.

Unlike a custodial prop firm, ordinary profit here is split between trader and builder by a pinned `builderPoolBps`. Vault and protocol take none of it. They earn from premium, subscription and forfeited surplus, not from denying a payout.

<Tip>
Profit the contract already credited does not belong to the firm. It cannot deny that payout outside protocol rules.
</Tip>

## How you get an account

A builder opens it, or the trader calls `activateFunded` (self-funded). How a builder selects people off-protocol (evaluation, their store) is their product, not the protocol.

The account is bound to the trader's HyperEVM address. Trading goes through an API wallet. The Exchange Proxy signs Hyperliquid. The trader never holds that key.

| | Sponsored | Self-funded |
|---|---|---|
| Who pays `R + P` | The caller. They are `record.builder` | The trader |
| Principal `A` | From MainVault | From MainVault |
| Daily drawdown | On | Off |
| Time | 30-day subscription, can renew | Prepaid 1 / 7 / 30 days |
| Ordinary profit | Split by `builderPoolBps` | Same formulas, both buckets to the trader |

Lifetime floor is the same. Self-funded has no daily floor, so the session path is wider.

The live MainVault list is the only market list. An edit does not close the account. Off-list entries are refused. Leftover size on a withdrawn market is flattened.

```
activate → Active → trade
                 → withdraw profit (optional)
                 → close → settle → claim
```

Close is one path. The clone snapshots actual Core trading capital `Q`. MainVault applies the frozen record and credits a pull ledger.
