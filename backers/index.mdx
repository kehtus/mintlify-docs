> The backer owns the product. Proportion provides the funded layer.

The funded account is the most capital-intensive part of a prop firm. Proportion funds it, enforces its floor and settles it. Evaluation, brand, marketing and trader selection stay your product.

The capital comes from the vault. You pay `B + P` to activate an account, and whatever part of `B` the trader does not lose comes back, less any [vault debt repayment](/backers/fees#vault-debt-repayment). In a prop firm that money usually comes from fees on challenges that did not pass.

## What Proportion gives you

Activation opens an isolated account with its own capital and floor. It belongs to the trader and follows the protocol's rules.

| Your product | The protocol |
|--------------|--------------|
| Evaluation and selection | Capital from the LP vault |
| Trader onboarding | Floors, closes, allowed markets and leverage caps |
| Brand, marketing, pricing | Settlement: the premium, the subscription and the profit split |

You decide who receives an account and on what commercial terms.

## Who counts as the backer

On a direct activation, the wallet that pays `B + P` is the backer. To fund yourself, name your own address as the trader, and both the backer's and the trader's shares go to you. When an account opens from a challenge promotion or a finance listing, the backer is whoever the challenge or listing names.

## Where your revenue comes from

| Source | What it is |
|---|---|
| Your share of trader profit | The share you fix at activation, taken from every profit withdrawal and every ordinary close. See [Profit and Payouts](/funded/payouts) |
| Forfeited profit | When the account closes on a drawdown breach or the trader's own request, you take **half** of the profit left in it. See [Account Parameters and Fees](/backers/fees#what-comes-back) |
| Evaluation margin | Evaluation revenue after any checkout fees and the cost of funding the traders you select. See [Challenges](/challenges) |
| LP yield | Depositing into the vault earns LP shares. You do not need them to open accounts, but you do to publish and sell challenge plans. See [Yield and Risks](/vault/yield) |

The subscription is not backer revenue: it splits between the vault and the protocol.
