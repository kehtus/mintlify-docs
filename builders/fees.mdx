## What the builder picks

| Parameter | Corridor | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `trader` | any address | Who the account is opened for. If that is the caller, the path is self-funded |
| `accountSize` | $10-$20,000 | Principal `A` |
| `drawdownBps` | 5%-25% | Trailing `D`. Sets reserve `R` |
| `builderPoolBps` | 10%-70% | Builder share of ordinary profit |
| `targetProfit` | ≥ 0 | Absolute TARGET. `0` disables |
| `feeBps` | 200 / 400 / 800 | Sponsored: **200** only. Self-funded picks the term |
| `proposedReferrer` | optional | Binds the caller |

Daily drawdown is not a parameter. Sponsored has it. Self-funded does not.

`MIN/MAX_ACCOUNT_SIZE` and `MIN/MAX_DRAWDOWN_BPS` can move through `setRiskConfig`.

## Activation charge

```
R = floor(A × D / 10_000)     // builder reserve
P = ceil(A × F / 10_000)      // premium from A, not from R
charge = R + P
```

Raising `D` means posting more refundable reserve. Premium does not grow with it.

| Path | `F` | Term |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsored | 2% | 30 days, renewing subscription |
| Self-funded T1 | 2% | 1 day prepaid |
| Self-funded T2 | 4% | 7 days prepaid |
| Self-funded T3 | 8% | 30 days prepaid |

```
protocol premium = floor(P × 50%)
vault premium    = P − protocol premium
```

The caller pays `R + P` in USDC. MainVault places exactly `A` as principal.

Example: `A = $1,000`, `D = 10%`, sponsored 2% → `R = $100`, `P = $20`, charge `$120`. Vault and protocol take `$10` of premium each. Reserve `$100`. Allocation `$1,000`.

## Allocation limits

A vault deposit is not required to activate. The vault itself caps volume:

| Limit | Default | What it cuts |
|---|---|---|
| Max utilization | 80% of NAV | Vault share in active accounts |
| Single account | 10% of NAV, cap $20k | One `A` |

At defaults you cannot activate until NAV is at least $100. Deposits below that still work.

## Subscription

```
subscriptionFee = ceil_to_1_USDC(A × 1.5%)
```

Split 50/50 vault / protocol. Builder gets 0.

Sponsored accounts renew from Core equity in the last 3 days of the period, if the fee can be taken without breaching floors. Self-funded does not renew that way. Extra time is `extendRental` (same tiers, no new `R`, horizon now + 30 days).

## Profit and close

Live withdrawal and a non-forfeiting close use `builderPoolBps`. A forfeiting close (`Drawdown`, `TraderRequested`) zeros unwithdrawn `Q − A` and splits that surplus 40 / 20 / LP residual. Unused `R` returns to `reserveRecipient`.

Admin close keeps the ordinary split. That is break-glass, not a forfeit.

Hyperliquid builder-code fees from a funded account go to `protocolFeeReceiver`, not the builder.
