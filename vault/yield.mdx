Vault yield is residual. It can be zero.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Activation premium" icon="percent">
    **50%** of `P`. `P` is a share of account size, not of the reserve
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscription" icon="repeat">
    **50%** of sponsored renewals (1.5% of `A` per period). Self-funded prepaid premium uses the same 50/50
  </Card>
  <Card title="Forfeited surplus" icon="chart-line">
    Residual of `Q − A` after builder 40% and protocol 20%, on Drawdown or TraderRequested
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

LPs do not take ordinary trader / builder profit. Unused `R` returns to the builder, not to the vault.

A loss beyond reserve becomes `reserveDeficit` in NAV. Settlement still completes. First loss is `R`.

## Risks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Correlated drawdowns" icon="waves">
    `P` is small relative to allocated `A`. Simultaneous breaches can eat accumulated vault income and push `sharePrice` below the deposit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Gap / overshoot" icon="zap">
    Floors start a close. They do not guarantee a fill at the floor. A Core liquidation or gap can realize `Q` below the floor. Excess beyond `R` hits LPs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Issuance quality" icon="users">
    LPs do not pick traders. A weak sponsor hits the pool in proportion to allocated principal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Smart contract" icon="file-code">
    MainVault and FundedAccount were independently reviewed. Residual implementation risk remains.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Hyperliquid / Hypercore" icon="link">
    Equity, fills and bridges depend on Hypercore reads. Faults there show up as vault capital.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LP exit" icon="lock">
    Open accounts do not lock shares. Exits still cannot break utilization: `totalAllocated` must stay within 80% of post-withdraw NAV.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Governance" icon="settings">
    Account size, drawdown and utilization bands can move through `setRiskConfig` inside published bounds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
