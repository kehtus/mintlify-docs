> You build the product. Proportion supplies the funded layer: capital, ready to use.

For a builder, the funded account is the most capital-heavy and infrastructure-heavy part of a prop firm. Proportion ships it as a service: capital, an on-chain risk engine, settlement. Selection, brand, marketing and off-protocol price stay yours.

The builder buys capital from the protocol: reserve `R` plus premium `P`. Unused reserve returns at close. `builder` is whoever paid. There is no registry. A vault deposit is not required. Any wallet with USDC for `R + P` can open an account for themselves or someone else.

The line is simple. Everything before `activateFunded` is yours. After activation, the protocol protects the capital.

<Warning>
Hyperliquid builder-code fees from a funded account go to `protocolFeeReceiver`, not the builder.
</Warning>

## What the protocol supplies

| Builder layer | Protocol layer |
|---|---|
| Selection and brand | Principal from MainVault |
| Off-protocol program price | Floors, TARGET, live whitelist |
| How you find traders | Reserve refund, ordinary split, forfeited surplus |

A funded account is an isolated clone with principal `A`, reserve `R` and automatic risk enforcement. The account belongs to the trader inside protocol rules.

## Builder P&L

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unused reserve" icon="shield">
    `R` is yours. Actual principal loss consumes it 1:1. The rest returns
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ordinary profit" icon="split">
    `builderPoolBps` of keep-profit withdrawals and non-forfeiting closes (10-70%)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Forfeited surplus" icon="percent">
    40% of actual `Q − A` after Drawdown or TraderRequested
  </Card>
  <Card title="LP shares" icon="trending-up">
    Optional. Same residual vault income, same losses. Activation does not need shares
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Builders do not take subscription. That split is 50/50 vault / protocol.

First loss is reserve `R`, not a second vault deposit. Volume is bounded by the vault: 80% utilization, one account at most 10% of NAV.

More on vault yield and risk: [Vault](/vault/yield).
