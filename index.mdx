![Proportion overview](/images/image.jpeg)

Proportion is a trader-financing protocol on HyperEVM.

Three sides. Traders operate isolated funded accounts on Hyperliquid. Builders write the terms, the contracts enforce them. LPs deposit USDC into MainVault and take a share of vault income. Yield is not promised and can be zero.

The hook is `FundedAccount`. Capital sits in an isolated clone under pinned rules. Fills are real Hypercore executions. The trader operates that account. They do not own the vault and cannot take principal home.

MainVault holds LP capital, allocates principal, books builder reserve, and runs one close path. The account trades only perps on the live whitelist. Drawdown, TARGET, time, withdrawals, and claims are contract state.
