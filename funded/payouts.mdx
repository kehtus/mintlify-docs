---
title: "Profit and Payouts"
description: "How your profit is split, how you withdraw it, and what you receive when the account closes"
---

When your account settles, the vault credits your payout to your address. Anyone can then call `claimPayout` to send it, but only to that address, and nobody's approval is needed.

## Your split

One number decides it: the backer's share, written into your account at activation.

| Backer | You |
|---|---|
| 10 % | **90 %** |
| 20 % | **80 %** |
| 70 % | **30 %** |

Your share is between 30 % and 90 %, and nothing changes it after activation. It applies to every withdrawal and every ordinary close.

<Note>
If you were quoted 80 %, that number comes from the backer's plan, not from the protocol. Ask your backer for your share before you accept.
</Note>

The vault and the protocol take nothing from a withdrawal or an ordinary close. On a self-funded account both shares land in the same wallet, so you keep all of it.

## Withdrawing profit

Profit is your current equity above account size, and every withdrawal takes all of it. You can withdraw before the account closes only if the account has no profit target. You need at least \$1 of profit and either route below.

| Route | Condition |
|---|---|
| Day count | Three days when equity is at least 0.7 % above the previous day. They need not be consecutive |
| 1.5 % route | Profit of at least 1.5 % of account size, with no day count at all |

When you request, the account must be flat and in good standing: active, paid up, not below its floors, with no close request or subscription payment pending, and with enough USDC on HyperCore to cover the profit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="You request">
    Only you can request it. On the Proportion frontend the gas is paid for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The profit bridges">
    Your profit moves from HyperCore to HyperEVM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Anyone claims">
    The claim needs no permission. Your share goes to your address, and the backer's share is credited to theirs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A completed withdrawal resets your floors and your profitable-day count. With a \$1,000 account and 10 % drawdown, the trailing floor returns to \$900 and the backed account's daily floor to \$950.
</Warning>

## What you receive at each closure

For a backed account:

| Why the account closed | Profit still in the account |
|---|---|
| Drawdown breach | Split in half between backer and protocol |
| You asked to close | Split in half between backer and protocol |
| Subscription expired | Your ordinary split |
| Subscription cancelled | Your ordinary split |
| Admin closure | Your ordinary split |
| Profit target reached | Your ordinary split |

A drawdown breach and a close at your request are forfeiting closes. If the account closes at or below its size, there is no profit to divide, and the loss comes out of the backer's buffer before LP capital.

On a self-funded account you are also the backer. On a forfeiting close you get the backer's half and the protocol gets the other half. On an ordinary close both shares are yours. Your unused buffer also comes back to you, though after a forfeiting close the vault may keep part of it while it is in debt. See [Vault debt](/vault/yield#vault-debt).

<Warning>
Closing the account yourself forfeits your share of the profit still in it, and you can ask only while the account is flat. So if the account has no profit target and qualifies for a withdrawal, withdraw and claim first, then ask to close. With a profit target, your share is paid only through an ordinary close.
</Warning>

## What a close cannot touch

Profit already in a pending withdrawal is paid under your ordinary split, whatever the close reason. In the rare case that this transfer still has not arrived a week after the close begins, the missing part can lose its ordinary split.
