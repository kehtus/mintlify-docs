---
title: "Activation"
description: "How to open the first funded account"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wallet">
    Any EVM wallet on HyperEVM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    `activateFunded(trader, accountSize, drawdownBps, builderPoolBps, targetProfit, proposedReferrer, feeBps)`. You pay `R + P`. The vault allocates principal `A`. The caller becomes `record.builder`. Sponsored `feeBps` must be 200.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If `trader` is you, that is self-funded: same call, tier 200 / 400 / 800. A vault deposit is not required on either path.

There are no delegates. Gasless submission is the ERC-2771 Forwarder: the signer pays USDC and becomes the builder.

`proposedReferrer` binds the caller, not the trader.

<Warning>
Depositing USDC as an LP is a separate role. Shares do not unlock or cap activation. They take residual vault income and can fall.
</Warning>
